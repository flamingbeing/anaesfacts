# Regional Anaesthesia Equipment

Infusion recipes for regional catheters, PCA pump settings for intermittent bolus, and common needles and catheters.

## Truncal catheter elastomeric pump
- Baxter elastomeric multirate infusor
- Dilute 60 ml of 1% ropivacaine (600 mg) in 240 ml normal saline
- Gives 600 mg ropivacaine in 300 ml (0.2%)
- Run at 5, 7 or 12 ml/h

## Epidural catheter infusion bag
- Dilute 120 ml of 1% ropivacaine (1200 mg, 6 ampoules) in 480 ml normal saline
  - Draw 20 ml out of a 500 ml bag first
- Gives 1200 mg ropivacaine in 600 ml (0.2%)

## PCA pump set-up for an ESP catheter
- Menu path: Task, then Admin setting, then Delivery, then Intermittent bolus and General
- Intermittent bolus: set delivery limit to 30 ml per 4 h interval
- General: set the hourly limit to 30 ml

## Needles and catheters
- Paediatric epidural (Perifix ONE): 18G 50 mm Tuohy needle with 20G 100 cm catheter
- Catheters can be tunnelled using a Tuohy needle
- Pajunk 22G, 50 or 80 mm
- Pajunk 21G, 100 mm
- Pajunk Tuohy 18G 5 cm (nanoline)
- Contiplex Tuohy Ultra 10 cm
- Spinal: introducer needle with Whitacre 25G

## Flashcards
- Q: How is a 0.2% ropivacaine elastomeric pump for a truncal catheter made up?
  - A: 60 ml of 1% ropivacaine (600 mg) in 240 ml normal saline, total 300 ml
- Q: What rates does the multirate truncal catheter infusor offer?
  - A: 5, 7 or 12 ml/h
- Q: How is a 600 ml 0.2% ropivacaine epidural bag made up?
  - A: Remove 20 ml from a 500 ml saline bag, then add 120 ml of 1% ropivacaine (1200 mg)
- Q: What PCA limits are used for intermittent bolus via an ESP catheter?
  - A: 30 ml per 4 h interval, and 30 ml per hour limit
