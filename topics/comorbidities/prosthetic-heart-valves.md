# Prosthetic Heart Valves

Key issues: valve function sets the haemodynamic goals, and anticoagulation (bridging) must be managed; neuraxial blocks are contraindicated while therapeutically anticoagulated.

## Preoperative assessment

- Valve details: type (tilting disc, bileaflet, caged-ball or bioprosthetic), position (mitral, aortic, tricuspid), date implanted, structural integrity
  - Check CXR
- Echo (TTE or TOE)
  - Valve function
    - Transvalvular gradients, effective orifice area (EOA), patient-prosthesis mismatch (PPM)
    - Paravalvular leak (PVL)
    - Thrombus
  - Ventricular function: LV ejection fraction, LV hypertrophy and compliance, RV function, PASP
- Comorbidities: rhythm (AF burden and rate control), degree of secondary pulmonary hypertension

## Anticoagulation management

- Mechanical valves are highly thrombogenic
- High thrombotic risk
  - Mitral or tricuspid mechanical valve
  - Older-generation (caged-ball) valve
  - Multiple valves
  - Previous VTE or stroke
  - AF
  - LVEF < 35%
  - Aortic valves are lower risk, especially without other risk factors
- Bridging needed if high risk
  - Stop warfarin 5 days before surgery
  - Start therapeutic UFH or LMWH once INR < 2.0
  - Stop LMWH 24 hours before surgery; stop IV UFH 4–6 hours before
  - Check INR before surgery (target < 1.5 for major surgery)
- Neuraxial blocks
  - Strictly contraindicated during therapeutic bridging
  - Observe guideline time windows (ASRA/ESRA): e.g. 24 hours after therapeutic LMWH; normal aPTT after UFH

## Intraoperative management

- Infective endocarditis prophylaxis only for high-risk procedures (e.g. dental work with gingival manipulation) in high-risk patients
- Arterial line before induction, with or without CVP
  - Low threshold for intraoperative TOE to check leaflet movement
- Slow, titrated, cardiostable induction
- Haemodynamic goals set by valve function and pathology

## Flashcards
- Q: When is warfarin stopped before surgery in a patient with a mechanical valve?
  - A: 5 days before
- Q: When is bridging LMWH started and stopped?
  - A: Started once INR < 2.0; stopped 24 hours before surgery
- Q: When is IV UFH stopped before surgery?
  - A: 4–6 hours before
- Q: Target preoperative INR for major surgery?
  - A: < 1.5
- Q: Name features of high thrombotic risk with a prosthetic valve.
  - A: Mitral or tricuspid mechanical valve, caged-ball valve, multiple valves, previous VTE or stroke, AF, LVEF < 35%
