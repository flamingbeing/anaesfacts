# Panhypopituitarism

Loss of anterior and posterior pituitary hormones (e.g. after adenoma resection or a non-functioning adenoma); needs endocrinology referral for replacement.

## Hormone replacement
- Refer to an endocrinologist (e.g. after adenoma resection, or non-functioning adenoma)
- Hydrocortisone: ACTH deficiency, adrenal insufficiency
- Thyroxine: TSH deficiency, hypothyroidism
- Testosterone: gonadotrophin (GnRH axis) deficiency, male hypogonadism
- Oestradiol and progesterone: gonadotrophin (GnRH axis) deficiency, female hypogonadism
- Growth hormone: by injection
- DDAVP nasal spray: ADH deficiency, central diabetes insipidus

## Flashcards
- Q: Replacement for ACTH deficiency in panhypopituitarism?
  - A: Hydrocortisone
- Q: Replacement for ADH deficiency?
  - A: DDAVP nasal spray (central diabetes insipidus)
- Q: Replacement for TSH deficiency?
  - A: Thyroxine
