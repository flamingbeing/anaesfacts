# Guillain-Barré Syndrome

Acute neuromuscular weakness: risk of respiratory failure and aspiration; avoid suxamethonium.

## Preoperative assessment
- May need ventilatory support
- Increased aspiration risk

## Anaesthetic management
- Consider rapid sequence induction (RSI)
- Avoid suxamethonium (succinylcholine)
- Minimise muscle relaxants and opioids
