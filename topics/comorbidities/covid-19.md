# COVID-19

Follow current infection control guidance, judge infectivity, and time elective surgery after vaccination or infection.

## General
- Infection control as per current institutional guidance; ask infectious diseases if unsure

## Preoperative assessment
- Infectivity: COVID PCR cycle threshold (CT) value
- Complications of the infection
- Timing of elective surgery
  - >2 weeks after a vaccine or booster dose
  - >3 weeks after mild infection
  - >7 weeks after moderate to severe infection needing oxygen

## Intraoperative management
- Airborne precautions

## Flashcards
- Q: Delay for elective surgery after moderate to severe COVID-19 needing oxygen?
  - A: >7 weeks
- Q: Delay after mild COVID-19?
  - A: >3 weeks
- Q: Delay after a vaccine or booster dose?
  - A: >2 weeks
