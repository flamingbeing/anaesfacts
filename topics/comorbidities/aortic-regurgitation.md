# Aortic Regurgitation

Haemodynamic goal "fast, full, forward": keep heart rate high-normal, preload normal and SVR low-normal so blood keeps moving forward.

## Severity and guidelines
- Severity grading per the 2020 AHA valve guideline
- Symptomatic severe AR meeting criteria for valve intervention: intervene before elective non-cardiac surgery (2024 AHA)

## Preoperative assessment
- Risk stratification
  - Symptomatic with functional capacity < 4 METS (cannot climb a flight of stairs or walk on the flat at > 6 km/h): aortic valve replacement before elective surgery
  - Asymptomatic with good exercise tolerance: manage to haemodynamic goals
- Continue antiarrhythmics
- Consider premedication to avoid sympathetic rises in SVR

## Intraoperative management
- Monitoring
  - 5-lead ECG with standard ASA monitoring (NIBP, SpO2)
  - Arterial line before induction for major surgery
  - TEE and other cardiac output monitors are inaccurate in AR
- Technique: neuraxial blocks are well tolerated
- Haemodynamic goals
  - Heart rate high-normal, 80-90 bpm; treat persistent bradycardia with a beta-agonist or anticholinergic
  - Normal preload
  - SVR low-normal while keeping diastolic pressure up
    - Afterload reduction helps only some patients: low EF, low CO, raised LVEDP, systemic hypertension
    - Treat hypotension with mixed alpha and beta agonists (ephedrine, adrenaline, dopamine, dobutamine) rather than pure alpha agonists (phenylephrine), which raise SVR and regurgitant volume
  - Normal contractility
  - Keep sinus rhythm: treat SVT or AF with hypotension promptly by synchronised DC cardioversion

## Cardiopulmonary bypass
- Starting bypass is critical: bradycardia or VF lets the LV fill rapidly through the leaking valve
  - Pace, defibrillate or cross-clamp to stop LV distension
- Myocardial protection is harder
  - Aortic root pressure is too low for antegrade cardioplegia
  - Give cardioplegia via aortotomy with direct coronary ostial cannulation, or retrogradely
- Inotropes often needed after AVR: the low-impedance run-off through the leaking valve is gone, so LV work rises
- With an aortic suture line, treat hypertension fast to avoid bleeding and dissection

## Flashcards
- Q: Haemodynamic goals in aortic regurgitation?
  - A: Fast (HR 80-90 bpm), full (normal preload), forward (low-normal SVR), normal contractility, sinus rhythm
- Q: Preferred treatment of hypotension in AR?
  - A: Mixed alpha and beta agonist (e.g. ephedrine), not phenylephrine, which increases regurgitant volume
- Q: Why is starting bypass risky in AR?
  - A: Bradycardia or VF lets the LV distend through the incompetent valve
- Q: Which symptomatic AR patients need AVR before elective surgery?
  - A: Those with functional capacity < 4 METS
