# Altered mental status, dementia and delirium

Main issues: consent, cooperation at induction and emergence, and the risk of postoperative cognitive decline.

## Key issues
- Consent
- May not cooperate at induction or emergence (manage like a child)
- Risk of postoperative cognitive dysfunction (POCD)

## Confusion alone
- Preoperative: patient may lack capacity, so consent needs a substitute process
- Aim to avoid worsening the dementia (see postoperative delirium and POCD)

## Reduced or borderline GCS
- May not be safe to extubate after surgery

## Flashcards
- Q: Three main anaesthetic issues in a confused or demented patient?
  - A: Consent, poor cooperation at induction and emergence, risk of POCD
- Q: Concern after surgery in a patient with borderline GCS?
  - A: May not be extubatable
- Q: Approach to induction and emergence in an uncooperative confused adult?
  - A: Manage as for a child
