# Hyperlipidaemia

Marker of ischaemic heart disease risk; the main perioperative point is to keep statins going.

## Anaesthetic considerations
- Continue statins
- Treat as an ischaemic heart disease risk factor

## Statins
- Continue through the perioperative period if already taking them
- Stopping them abruptly around surgery is linked to more cardiovascular complications
- Likely benefit comes from pleiotropic effects:
  - Less inflammation
  - Plaque stabilisation

## Flashcards
- Q: Should statins be stopped before surgery?
  - A: No; continue them perioperatively, as acute withdrawal increases cardiovascular morbidity
- Q: What are the proposed protective mechanisms of perioperative statins?
  - A: Pleiotropic effects: reduced inflammation and plaque stabilisation
- Q: What risk does hyperlipidaemia flag in a preoperative patient?
  - A: Ischaemic heart disease
