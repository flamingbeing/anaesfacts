# Lumbar Spinal Stenosis

Narrowing of the lumbar canal; mainly matters for neuraxial anaesthesia and documentation of existing deficits.

## Anaesthetic considerations
- Neuraxial anaesthesia may be difficult or contraindicated after previous spinal instrumentation or with neurological deficits
- Document existing back pain and neurological deficits before anaesthesia

## Flashcards
- Q: When may neuraxial anaesthesia be difficult or contraindicated in lumbar spinal stenosis?
  - A: After previous spinal instrumentation or with neurological deficits
- Q: What must be documented before anaesthesia?
  - A: Existing back pain and neurological deficits
- Q: What features of lumbar spinal stenosis should be documented preoperatively?
  - A: Back pain and neurological deficits
