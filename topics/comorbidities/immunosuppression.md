# Immunosuppression

Anaesthesia for immunosuppressed patients (e.g. transplant recipients): asepsis, steroid cover, drug side effects, blood products.

## Key points
- Strict asepsis
- Complications of immunosuppression, e.g. renal impairment
- Stress-dose steroids
- Leukoreduced, with or without irradiated, red cells

## Immune-related risks
- Short term
  - Staphylococcal mediastinitis
  - CMV reactivation
  - Toxoplasma gondii
- Long term: infection
  - Intracellular bacteria (Listeria, non-typhoid salmonella, mycobacteria)
  - Legionella and Pneumocystis
  - Invasive aspergillosis
  - Candida

## Other long-term risks
- Squamous cell carcinoma of skin
- Lymphoma (EBV associated)
- Musculoskeletal problems (osteoporosis)
- Skin fragility
- Nephrotoxicity (ciclosporin)
- Ischaemic heart disease from steroids
- Hypertension (ciclosporin)
- Epilepsy
- Pancreatitis
- Cholelithiasis
- Gingival hyperplasia
- Higher incidence of pre-eclampsia (PET)

## Drug-specific issues
- Steroids
  - HPA axis suppression
- Ciclosporin
  - Renal dysfunction and hypertension
  - Prolongs action of non-depolarising NMBs
  - Calcium channel blockers markedly raise ciclosporin levels: avoid starting or changing their dose perioperatively if possible
- Tacrolimus
  - Renal dysfunction

## Anaesthetic management
- Strict asepsis for all invasive procedures, including IV cannulation
- Consider HPA axis suppression and the need for stress-dose steroids if on steroids previously
- Consider organ side effects (e.g. nephrotoxicity); check baseline function
- Transfusion: may need leukodepleted, with or without irradiated, blood products

## Flashcards
- Q: How does a calcium channel blocker interact with ciclosporin?
  - A: It markedly raises ciclosporin levels; avoid starting or changing it perioperatively if possible.
- Q: What effect does ciclosporin have on non-depolarising NMBs?
  - A: Prolongs their action.
- Q: What blood products may an immunosuppressed patient need?
  - A: Leukodepleted, with or without irradiated, products.
- Q: Which two immunosuppressants commonly cause renal dysfunction?
  - A: Ciclosporin and tacrolimus.
