# Psychiatric Patients

Schizophrenia, depression and anxiety: consent, anxiety, and perioperative handling of psychotropic drugs.

## Key issues
- Consent
- Anxiety
- Psychotropic interactions:
  - QTc prolongation and ondansetron
  - Serotonin syndrome with tramadol or pethidine
  - Extrapyramidal effects with metoclopramide
  - Withdrawal
- Psychotropics, often combined with each other or other drugs, act strongly on central and peripheral neurotransmitters

## Perioperative psychotropic drugs
- TCAs (amitriptyline, imipramine, dosulepin)
  - Concerns: muscarinic, histamine and alpha-adrenergic block; QTc prolongation
  - Withdrawal: yes
  - Advice: stop
- SSRIs (venlafaxine, fluoxetine)
  - Concerns: anticholinergic effect; avoid serotonin syndrome triggers; QTc prolongation
  - Withdrawal: yes
  - Advice: can continue
- MAOIs (phenelzine, moclobemide)
  - Concerns: avoid indirect-acting sympathomimetics; avoid serotonin syndrome triggers
  - Withdrawal: yes
  - Advice: irreversible MAOI, stop 2 weeks before surgery; reversible MAOI, stop on the day of surgery
- Lithium
  - Concerns: prolongs neuromuscular blockers; lowers anaesthetic requirement; avoid NSAIDs
  - Withdrawal: no
  - Advice: stop 24 h before surgery
- Carbamazepine
  - Concerns: induces cytochrome P450
  - Withdrawal: no
  - Advice: can continue
- Valproate
  - Concerns: impairs platelet function
  - Withdrawal: no
  - Advice: can continue
- Typical antipsychotics (prochlorperazine, chlorpromazine)
  - Concerns: cholinergic, alpha-1 and histamine block; seizures reported with desflurane
  - Withdrawal: yes
  - Advice: can continue
- Atypical antipsychotics (quetiapine, risperidone)
  - Concerns: alpha-1 block
  - Withdrawal: yes
  - Advice: can continue
- Benzodiazepines (lorazepam, temazepam)
  - Concerns: sedation
  - Withdrawal: yes
  - Advice: can continue

## Flashcards
- Q: When should an irreversible MAOI be stopped before surgery?
  - A: 2 weeks before; a reversible MAOI on the day of surgery
- Q: When should lithium be stopped and why?
  - A: 24 h before surgery; it prolongs neuromuscular block and lowers anaesthetic requirement
- Q: Which analgesics can trigger serotonin syndrome?
  - A: Tramadol and pethidine
- Q: Which psychotropic class is stopped before surgery besides MAOIs and lithium?
  - A: TCAs
