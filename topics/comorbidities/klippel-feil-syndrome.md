# Klippel-Feil Syndrome

Congenital fusion of cervical vertebrae: expect a short stiff neck and difficult intubation, and check for cervical instability.

## Background
- Failure of formation or segmentation of cervical vertebrae
- Classic triad: short neck, low posterior hairline, limited neck movement; fewer than 50% have all three
- Mostly sporadic; both dominant and recessive inheritance reported
  - Types I and III: usually autosomal dominant in families
  - Type II: usually autosomal recessive

## Types
- Type I: extensive fusion of cervical and upper thoracic vertebrae into blocks; often with other severe syndromic anomalies
- Type II: fusion at only one or two cervical levels
  - C2/3 most common
  - Often other skeletal anomalies: Sprengel's deformity (congenitally high scapula), cervical rib, hemivertebrae, occipitoatlantal fusion
- Type III: cervical plus lower thoracic or lumbar fusion
- Types I and III: higher risk of scoliosis developing and progressing (60% patients)

## Associated anomalies
- Spina bifida (45%)
- Profound deafness (30%)
- Rib anomalies such as fused ribs (30%)
- Cleft lip or palate (15%)
- Renal aplasia and other genitourinary anomalies
- Congenital heart disease (10%, usually VSD)
- Less common: cervical rib, micrognathia, syringomyelia, Moebius syndrome (congenital facial palsy with weak eye abduction), soft tissue webbing (neck, syndactyly), platybasia
- Present at birth but often diagnosed later

## Anaesthetic considerations
- Typical operations: excision of fused or cervical ribs, lowering a raised scapula, genitourinary surgery, neurosurgery for instability or cord compression
- Undiagnosed cases may present as an unexpected difficult intubation (short stiff neck)
- Known cases: identify fused levels and assess cervical stability
  - Instability risks cord injury during neck manipulation for intubation
  - MRI if there are signs or symptoms of cord compression
- Manage as a difficult airway
  - Fibreoptic intubation may be needed
  - LMAs have worked well with little neck movement
- Antibiotic prophylaxis may be needed if there is congenital heart disease
