# Eczema and Dermatological Conditions

Check steroid use, and protect thin skin from tape injury.

## Preoperative assessment
- Ask about topical steroid use
- Make sure there is no systemic steroid use (steroid cover may be needed)

## Intraoperative management
- Itch may stop the patient keeping still under sedation
- Thin skin: take care with tapes (e.g. ETT tape, eye tape)

## Flashcards
- Q: What drug history matters most in a patient with eczema?
  - A: Steroid use: topical, and make sure there is no systemic steroid use
- Q: Why may sedation alone be difficult in eczema?
  - A: Itch may stop the patient keeping still
- Q: What intraoperative care does thin skin need?
  - A: Care with tapes such as ETT and eye tape
