# Marfan Syndrome

Inherited fibrillin-1 connective tissue disorder: echo the aortic root before surgery, control BP, protect lax joints and keep airway pressures low.

## Overview

- Mutation in the fibrillin-1 gene on chromosome 15; collagen has less tensile strength
- Incidence about 1:10000 live births
- Autosomal dominant; about 25% are new mutations
- Main systems: skeletal, ocular, cardiovascular; very variable expression
- Diagnosis: clinical features plus family history
  - No family history: skeletal involvement plus at least two other systems, with at least one major feature (ectopia lentis, aortic dilatation or dissection, dural ectasia)
  - Family history: two systems are enough

## Clinical features

- Skeletal: tall and thin, arachnodactyly, joint laxity, pectus excavatum, scoliosis with or without kyphosis (10%), narrow high-arched palate with crowded teeth
- Ocular: lens subluxation (ectopia lentis), myopia (long globe), retinal detachment
- Cardiovascular
  - Mitral valve prolapse, mitral regurgitation (commonest reason for cardiac surgery), aortic root dilatation, aortic regurgitation
  - Aortic aneurysm and dissection: commonest cause of death
  - Pregnant patients at particular risk in the third stage of labour
  - About one-third have MVP, root dilatation or both on echo despite a normal examination
- Other
  - Pulmonary blebs (spontaneous pneumothorax)
  - Dural ectasia (widened spinal canal eroding the sacrum); spinal arachnoid cysts or diverticula
  - Obstructive sleep apnoea (lax pharyngeal wall)

## Anaesthetic considerations

- Cardiovascular
  - Echo before any surgery: aortic root dilatation, aortic regurgitation, MVP
  - Prophylactic beta-blockers slow aortic dilatation and improve survival
  - Labile BP during surgery: sodium nitroprusside or GTN infusion
  - Ascending aorta repair is common; composite valve and ascending aorta conduit for moderate AR or root >5.0-5.5 cm
  - Endocarditis risk: antibiotic prophylaxis
- Positioning: careful handling and padding of lax joints to avoid dislocation
- Ventilation: blebs raise pneumothorax and lung complication risk; keep peak airway pressures low
