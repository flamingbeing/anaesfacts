# Codeine

Weak opioid prodrug: about 10% becomes morphine via CYP2D6, so effect varies with genotype.

## Administration
- Oral, IM or rectal
- Not given IV: tends to cause hypotension, probably via histamine release

## Dosage
- Oral: 15–60 mg three times daily (max 60mg every 4 hours)
- IM: 30 or 60mg

## Mechanism of action
- Prodrug: about 10% converted to morphine
- 10 times less potent than morphine; unsuitable for severe pain

## Adverse effects
- Restricted in children under 12 years after reports of morphine toxicity in fast metabolisers
- IV use causes hypotension (histamine)

## Indications
- Analgesia (mild to moderate pain)
- Also used as antitussive, antidiarrhoeal, hypnotic and anxiolytic

## Pharmacokinetics
- Oral bioavailability 50%, slightly higher than morphine (methyl group reduces first-pass metabolism)
- 5–15% excreted unchanged in urine; the rest metabolised in the liver by three routes
  - 80% (60-80%): glucuronidation to codeine-6-glucuronide (CYP3A4); main clearance route
  - 10% (10-20%): N-demethylation to norcodeine (CYP3A4)
  - 10% (up to 15%): O-demethylation to morphine (CYP2D6)
    - Morphine then forms normorphine, morphine-3-glucuronide and morphine-6-glucuronide
- Active metabolites: morphine and morphine-6-glucuronide
- CYP2D6 is non-inducible and polymorphic
  - Poor metabolisers get little analgesia: about 9% of the UK population, 30% of Hong Kong Chinese
  - Fast metabolisers make more morphine and its side effects
- Glucuronides are renally excreted and accumulate in renal failure
  - Morphine-6-glucuronide is active, thought to be 45-360 times more potent than morphine, and accumulates in renal impairment
  - Codeine-6-glucuronide, norcodeine and morphine-3-glucuronide are inactive
  - Morphine excretion itself is little affected by renal impairment

## Flashcards
- Q: What fraction of codeine becomes morphine, and by which enzyme?
  - A: About 10%, by CYP2D6 O-demethylation
- Q: Why is codeine restricted under 12 years?
  - A: Ultra-fast CYP2D6 metabolisers produce toxic amounts of morphine
- Q: Maximum oral codeine dosing?
  - A: 60 mg every 4 hours
- Q: Proportion of CYP2D6 poor metabolisers?
  - A: About 9% in the UK, 30% in Hong Kong Chinese
- Q: Why is codeine not given IV?
  - A: Hypotension, probably from histamine release
