# Etoricoxib

Highly selective oral COX-2 inhibitor (a methylsulfone); brand Arcoxia.

## Dosage

- 60-120mg once daily in the morning
- Hepatic impairment
  - Mild (Child-Pugh 5-6): maximum 60mg once daily
  - Moderate (Child-Pugh 7-9): maximum 60mg every other day
  - Severe: no data

## Mechanism of action

- COX-1:COX-2 inhibition ratio 1:344

## Adverse effects

- Side effect profile is predictable
- Gastric irritation similar to placebo and clearly less than COX-1 inhibitors; this advantage lessens after 9–12 months of use
- Major vascular event risk (MI, stroke) probably similar to diclofenac (limited data)

## Indications

- Effective analgesia compared with COX-1 inhibitors

## Pharmacokinetics

- Oral bioavailability >95%
- Volume of distribution 1.5 l/kg; 90% protein bound
- Elimination half-life 22 hours
- Hepatic metabolism by cytochrome P450, mainly CYP3A4; other isoenzymes take over more when CYP3A4 is inhibited
- Weak inhibitor of CYP2D6, CYP3A and CYP2C9
- Half-life increases in hepatic failure; unchanged in isolated renal failure
