# Enoximone

Imidazolone inodilator that selectively and competitively inhibits phosphodiesterase III, raising cAMP to increase contractility and dilate vessels.

## Presentation

- Yellow IV solution, 5 mg/ ml, pH 12
- In propylene glycol and ethanol
- Store between 5°C and 8°C

## Administration

- Dilute with an equal volume of water or 0.9% saline, in **plastic** syringes (crystals form in glass)
- Given as an infusion
- **May take up to 30 minutes to act**, unlike catecholamines

## Dosage

- Optional loading dose 0.5 mg/kg, repeatable to a maximum of 3 mg/kg
- Infusion 5–20 mcg/kg/min
- Reduce dose in renal failure

## Mechanism of action

- Selective, competitive **PDE-III inhibitor**
- Blocks breakdown of cAMP (and possibly cGMP) in heart and vascular smooth muscle
- Myocardium: more cAMP increases the slow inward Ca2+ current, so more calcium is released from stores near the contractile proteins: positive inotropy
- Vascular smooth muscle: interferes with calcium influx, causing vasodilation

## Effects

- **Inodilator**: **positive inotrope and vasodilator**
- In heart failure: cardiac output increases by about 30%, end-diastolic filling pressures fall by about 35%
- Myocardial oxygen extraction ratio unchanged, as wall tension falls and coronary perfusion improves
- Blood pressure unchanged or **falls**
- Heart rate unchanged or **rises slightly**
- **Shortens refractoriness** of atria, AV node and ventricles; **arrhythmias** are rare

## Adverse effects

- Ischaemic heart disease: **lower coronary perfusion pressure and faster heart rate** may outweigh better myocardial flow and cause ischaemia
- Deranged LFTs and thrombocytopenia
- **Agranulocytosis** reported
- Wide therapeutic ratio; low toxicity risk

## Indications

- **Congestive heart failure**
- Low cardiac output states after cardiac surgery

## Pharmacokinetics

- Well absorbed orally but **extensive first-pass** metabolism makes oral use ineffective
- About 70% protein bound
- Hepatic metabolism to a **renally excreted active sulfoxide** with 10% of the parent activity and a terminal half-life of 7.5 hours
- Little excreted unchanged in urine
- Terminal half-life by infusion: 4.5 hours
