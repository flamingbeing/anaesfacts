# Baclofen

Antispasmodic acting at GABA-B receptors; best for pain linked to spasticity.

## Presentation

- Oral tablets of 5 mg and 10 mg

## Administration

- Oral: given TDS
  - Titrate every 3 days to effect
- Intrathecal: for severe spasticity
- Stop by slow taper, never abruptly

## Dosage

- Oral: maximum 80 mg/day
  - Adjust dose in renal impairment
- Intrathecal: initial trial dose of 50-100 mcg

## Mechanism of action

- Agonist at presynaptic GABA-B receptors, mainly in the spinal cord
- Inhibits monosynaptic and polysynaptic reflexes

## Adverse effects

- Sedation, dizziness, weakness
- Hypotension
- Nausea
- Constipation
- Transaminitis: monitor LFTs
- Withdrawal: hallucinations, seizures
- Intrathecal overdose: antidote is physostigmine 1 mg IV

## Indications

- Spasticity, and pain associated with it
