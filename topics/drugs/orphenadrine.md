# Orphenadrine

Oral muscle relaxant with anticholinergic side effects, often combined with paracetamol (Anarex).

## Administration
- Oral

## Dosage
- 100mg twice daily
- Combination products (e.g. Anarex): three to four times daily

## Adverse effects
- Anticholinergic: drowsiness, urinary retention, dry mouth

## Indications
- Used as a muscle relaxant

## Flashcards
- Q: Usual oral orphenadrine dose?
  - A: 100 mg twice daily
- Q: Main side effects of orphenadrine?
  - A: Anticholinergic: drowsiness, urinary retention, dry mouth
- Q: Which combination product contains orphenadrine?
  - A: Anarex, with paracetamol
