# Chloral hydrate

Old oral sedative-hypnotic, first made in 1832.

## Administration

- Oral; unpleasant taste

## Dosage

- 25-100 mg/kg orally
- May repeat after 30 min at 25-50 mg/kg, up to a total of 2 g or 100 mg/kg, whichever is less

## Adverse effects

- Gastric irritation

## Indications

- Formerly for short-term insomnia and sedation before minor medical or dental procedures
- Mostly replaced by barbiturates in the mid-20th century, then by benzodiazepines

## Pharmacokinetics

- Onset 45-60 min
- Duration 60-120 min
