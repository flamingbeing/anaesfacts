# Helium and Heliox

Inert, low-density gas; as heliox it improves flow through obstructed upper airways.

## Presentation

- Heliox (79% He, 21% O2): brown cylinders with white shoulders
- 100% helium: brown cylinders at 137 bar
- Does not support combustion

## Mechanism of action

- Lower density (and specific gravity) than air and oxygen
- In turbulent flow, velocity is higher with heliox

## Effects

- Less work of breathing and better oxygenation in upper airway obstruction (e.g. tumour)
- Higher-pitched voice (squeaky) because of the low density

## Indications

- Upper airway obstruction
- Deep diving, to avoid nitrogen narcosis
