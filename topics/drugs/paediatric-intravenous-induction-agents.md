# Paediatric intravenous induction agents

Propofol and ketamine in children: doses, benefits and pitfalls.

## Propofol
- Used for induction, maintenance and sedation
- Usually preferred to thiopental for short cases: better early recovery
- Induction dose
  - Infants 3-5 mg/kg
  - Children 1.5-2.5 mg/kg
  - Not recommended for induction under 1 month of age, or for maintenance under 3 years
- More hypotension, respiratory depression and pharyngeal/laryngeal reflex suppression than an equipotent thiopental dose
- Early recovery from redistribution; metabolism and elimination faster than thiopental
- Advantages
  - Eases LMA insertion
  - Rapid, clear-headed waking after short procedures
  - Antiemetic
- Drawbacks
  - Injection pain upsets most children; lidocaine (0.2 mg/kg) is usually added
  - Maintenance: plasma level cannot be measured, so unnoticed pump failure, disconnection or tissued cannula can cause awareness
  - Propofol infusion syndrome (metabolic acidosis, rhabdomyolysis, severe cardiac failure, cardiac arrest) reported after infusion for sedation of children in ICU; now contraindicated for that use

## Ketamine
- Phencyclidine derivative, NMDA receptor antagonist; racemic mixture or S(+)-enantiomer
- Dissociative anaesthesia: catalepsy, catatonia, amnesia; eyes may stay open with nystagmus
- Strong analgesic
- Routes: IV, IM, oral, intranasal (premedication)
- Good for induction in haemodynamically unstable patients
- Dosage
  - Induction 1-2 mg/kg IV, or 5-10 mg/kg IM (onset in 3-5 min)
  - Analgesia 0.25-0.5 mg/kg IV
- Neonates: larger apparent volume of distribution and lower clearance than older children
- IM route is very useful in some situations
- Postoperative pain: infusion or PCA with opioids, to modify opioid tolerance
- Preservative-free ketamine can be added to local anaesthetic for caudal epidural

## Flashcards
- Q: Propofol induction dose in infants and in children?
  - A: 3-5 mg/kg in infants, 1.5-2.5 mg/kg in children
- Q: Age limits for propofol in children?
  - A: Not for induction under 1 month or maintenance under 3 years
- Q: Ketamine induction dose IV and IM?
  - A: 1-2 mg/kg IV or 5-10 mg/kg IM (onset 3-5 min)
- Q: Dose of lidocaine added to propofol to reduce injection pain?
  - A: 0.2 mg/kg
