# Dopexamine

Synthetic dopamine analogue acting on β2 and D1 receptors; raises cardiac output and gut perfusion.

## Presentation

- 50 mg in 5 ml (pH 2.5), for IV use

## Dosage

- 0.5–6 mcg/kg/min

## Mechanism of action

- Stimulates β2 and dopamine D1 receptors; may block noradrenaline reuptake
- Minimal D2 and β1 effect; no alpha effect

## Effects

- Cardiovascular
  - Positive inotrope (via cardiac β2 receptors)
  - Cardiac output also rises from afterload reduction (peripheral β2), which may lower blood pressure
  - Small rise in coronary blood flow; myocardial oxygen extraction unchanged
  - Heart rate changes vary; arrhythmias rare
- Mesenteric and renal: gut and kidney blood flow increase (higher cardiac output, lower regional resistance); urine output increases
- Respiratory: bronchodilation (β2)

## Adverse effects

- Nausea and vomiting
- Tremor, headache

## Indications

- To improve cardiac output and mesenteric perfusion

## Pharmacokinetics

- Rapidly cleared; half-life 7 minutes
