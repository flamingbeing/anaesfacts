# Levosimendan

Calcium sensitiser: a novel inotrope and inodilator (like milrinone), with effects lasting days after a 24-hour infusion.

## Administration

- IV (oral route also exists)
- Infusion usually limited to 24 hours

## Dosage

- Loading dose: 6–24 mcg/kg over 10 min
- Infusion: 0.05–0.2 mcg/kg/min for 24 hours

## Mechanism of action

- Two mechanisms:
  1. Inotropy: binds troponin C in a calcium-dependent way, sensitising cardiac muscle to Ca2+
     - Contractility increases without a rise in intracellular calcium
     - Diastolic relaxation is not impaired
  2. Vasodilation: opens ATP-sensitive K+ channels in vascular smooth muscle
     - Lower preload and afterload reduce myocardial oxygen consumption
     - Coronary blood flow and myocardial oxygen supply increase
     - Renal blood flow increases
- Can be used with beta-blockers (no competition at the receptor)

## Effects

- Increases: cardiac output (Q), stroke volume, heart rate, coronary blood flow
- Decreases: SVR, systolic BP, PCWP, PA pressure, coronary vascular resistance, myocardial oxygen consumption
- Improved symptoms and BNP in heart failure (SURVIVE trial, 2007)
- Active metabolites peak around 72 hours after starting and keep the benefit going for at least 7 days

## Adverse effects

- Hypotension
- Dysrhythmias
- Headache
- Nausea and vomiting

## Indications

- Heart failure, acute and chronic
- Sepsis
- Myocardial dysfunction after resuscitation
- Perioperative optimisation of cardiac patients with cardiomyopathy

## Pharmacokinetics

- Absorption: IV or oral
- Distribution: highly protein bound; peak concentrations after 2 days of treatment
- Metabolism: hepatic; active metabolite with half-life of 70 hours
- Elimination: urine and faeces; adjust dose in renal impairment

## Flashcards
- Q: What are the two mechanisms of levosimendan?
  - A: Calcium sensitisation via troponin C (inotropy) and opening of ATP-sensitive K+ channels (vasodilation)
- Q: What is the levosimendan loading dose and infusion?
  - A: 6–24 mcg/kg over 10 min, then 0.05–0.2 mcg/kg/min for 24 hours
- Q: Why do levosimendan effects outlast the infusion?
  - A: Active metabolites (half-life 70 hours, peak about 72 hours) keep effects going for at least 7 days
- Q: Can levosimendan be used with beta-blockers?
  - A: Yes; it does not compete at the receptor
