# Eperisone

Oral antispasmodic, centrally acting muscle relaxant (brand Myonal).

## Dosage

- 50 mg PO three times daily (TDS)

## Mechanism of action

- Antispasmodic and muscle relaxant
- Suppresses mono- and multisynaptic reflexes by inhibiting alpha and gamma efferent neurons in the spinal cord and supraspinal structures
