# Atipamezole

Selective α2 antagonist that reverses clonidine and dexmedetomidine.

## Mechanism of action
- Selective α2-adrenoceptor antagonist
- Crosses the blood-brain barrier

## Indications
- Reversal of sedation and analgesia from clonidine and dexmedetomidine

## Pharmacokinetics
- Elimination half-life 2 hours, similar to dexmedetomidine, which is a clear clinical advantage
