# Amethocaine (tetracaine)

Ester local anaesthetic used for topical anaesthesia of the eye and skin (Ametop).

## Presentation

- Eye drops 0.5% and 1%
- Skin cream 4% (Ametop)

## Administration

- Eye drops: topical anaesthesia before a local anaesthetic eye block, or as the sole agent for lens surgery
- Cream: topical skin anaesthesia, used much like EMLA cream

## Adverse effects

- Eye drops: burning on first instillation
