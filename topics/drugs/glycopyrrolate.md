# Glycopyrrolate

Quaternary ammonium antimuscarinic: competitive antagonist at muscarinic acetylcholine receptors, so it blocks parasympathetic activity without central effects.

## Presentation

- 0.6 mg in 3 ml (0.2 mg/ml); given neat

## Dosage

- Usually 0.4 mg given with 2.5 mg neostigmine
- 0.01 mg/kg (10 mcg/kg; memory aid: about 10 times the fentanyl dose)
- Onset 1 min; duration 2–4 hours

## Mechanism of action

- Competitive antagonist at muscarinic acetylcholine receptors, inhibiting parasympathetic activity
- Quaternary ammonium: permanently ionised, positively charged and hydrophilic, so it behaves differently from the tertiary amines

## Effects

- CNS: does not cross the blood–brain barrier, so no central effects

## Indications

- Blocking the muscarinic side effects of anticholinesterases (with neostigmine)
- Bradycardia
- Antisialogogue premedication

## Pharmacokinetics

- Negligible gut absorption; oral bioavailability under 5%
- Minimal metabolism; 80% excreted unchanged in urine
