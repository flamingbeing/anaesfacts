# Sildenafil

Selective PDE5 inhibitor that enhances nitric oxide vasodilation; used for pulmonary arterial hypertension and erectile dysfunction.

## Administration
- Oral

## Dosage
- Pulmonary arterial hypertension: 20 mg TDS

## Mechanism of action
- Potent, specific PDE5 inhibitor: enhances the effect of endogenous NO
- Raised cGMP relaxes vascular smooth muscle in the pulmonary arteries and corpus cavernosum

## Indications
- Pulmonary arterial hypertension
- Erectile dysfunction

## Pharmacokinetics
- Rapid absorption; oral bioavailability 40%
- 95% protein bound; volume of distribution 100 litres
- Metabolised by CYP3A4 (less by CYP2C9) to an active N-demethylated metabolite giving about 35% of the effect
    - Consider dose change with CYP3A4 inducers or inhibitors
- Elimination half-life 3–5 hours; 80% excreted in faeces
- Significant renal or hepatic impairment increases AUC
