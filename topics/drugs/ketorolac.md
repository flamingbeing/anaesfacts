# Ketorolac

Non-selective COX inhibitor (acetic acid derivative): strong analgesic and antipyretic, weak anti-inflammatory.

## Administration

- Oral or parenteral (IV)

## Dosage

- IV: typically 30 mg, or 0.5 mg/kg up to 60 mg

## Mechanism of action

- Non-selective COX inhibitor

## Effects

- Potent analgesia
- Potent antipyretic
- Limited anti-inflammatory action
- Duration of action up to 6 hours

## Adverse effects

- Same side-effect profile as other NSAIDs

## Flashcards
- Q: What is the usual IV dose of ketorolac?
  - A: 30 mg, or 0.5 mg/kg up to 60 mg
- Q: Is ketorolac COX-selective?
  - A: No; it is a non-selective COX inhibitor
- Q: How long does ketorolac act?
  - A: Up to 6 hours
