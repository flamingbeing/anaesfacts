# Clonidine

Alpha-2 agonist for sedation and analgesia; less alpha-2 selective than dexmedetomidine, so more initial and rebound hypertension.

## Presentation

- Tablets 25–300 mcg
- Clear colourless injection, 150 mcg/ml
- Transdermal patch: takes 48 hours to reach therapeutic levels

## Administration

- IV
  - Onset 10 min (slow)
  - Can be given near the end of surgery for sedation and pain relief
- Oral: possible (unlike dexmedetomidine) because oral bioavailability is good
  - Onset 45–60 min (slow)
- IM (premedication)
- Intranasal (premedication)
  - Onset 30–60 min (slow)
- Rectal
- Duration 4-6 hrs

## Dosage

- IV 1 mcg/kg every 6-8 hours
- Oral 1 mcg/kg every 6-8 hours
- Reduce the dose in renal impairment

## Mechanism of action

- Alpha agonist with about 200 times more affinity for alpha-2 than alpha-1 receptors (α2:α1 ratio 200:1)
- Some studies describe it as a partial agonist

## Effects

- Sedation
- Analgesia
- Otherwise similar to dexmedetomidine

## Adverse effects

- Hypertension, both initial and rebound
  - Lower alpha-2 selectivity means alpha-1 effects: more transient hypertension after a bolus, and more rebound hypertension on stopping

## Pharmacokinetics

- Oral bioavailability nearly 100%
- Protein binding 20%; volume of distribution about 2 l/kg
- Elimination half-life 9 to 18 hours
- About 50% metabolised in the liver to inactive metabolites; the rest excreted unchanged in urine
