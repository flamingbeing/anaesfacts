# Melatonin

Sleep-promoting hormone; Circadin is the prolonged-release form, used short term for primary insomnia.

## Presentation

- Circadin: prolonged-release melatonin

## Mechanism of action

- Activity at MT1, MT2 and MT3 receptors is thought to promote sleep

## Adverse effects

- Main: drowsiness
- Uncommon: irritability, nervousness, insomnia, abnormal dreams, migraine, headache, hyperactivity, hypertension, dry mouth, nausea, abdominal pain, abnormal liver function tests

## Indications

- Monotherapy for short-term treatment of primary insomnia with poor sleep quality
