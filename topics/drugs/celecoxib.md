# Celecoxib

Selective COX-2 inhibitor (Celebrex) for arthritis and postoperative pain.

## Presentation
- 100 mg tablets

## Dosage
- 200 mg twice daily

## Mechanism of action
- COX-1:COX-2 inhibitory ratio of 1:30

## Adverse effects
- Ulcer complications: in the CLASS trial, similar rate to non-selective NSAIDs (possibly confounded by concurrent aspirin)
- Cardiovascular
  - CLASS trial: no rise in stroke or MI with celecoxib
  - Meta-analyses, however, show COX-2 inhibitors do increase coronary events
  - Rofecoxib's apparent harm in the VIGOR study may reflect its greater COX-2 selectivity compared with celecoxib
- Interactions: CYP2C9 inhibitors (omeprazole) raise and inducers (carbamazepine) lower plasma levels

## Contraindications
- Sulfonamide allergy (it contains a sulfonamide group)
- As with all COX-2 inhibitors, avoid in established or at-risk ischaemic heart disease or cerebrovascular disease

## Indications
- Chronic pain of osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis
- Effective for postoperative pain

## Pharmacokinetics
- Peak plasma level at 2–3 hours
- Elimination half-life 8–12 hours
- 97% protein bound; volume of distribution 5.7 l/kg
- Metabolised by hepatic CYP2C9 to inactive metabolites; little appears unchanged in urine
