# Flumazenil

Imidazobenzodiazepine that competitively antagonises benzodiazepines; short-acting, so re-sedation is a risk.

## Administration

- IV in increments, titrated to effect to avoid withdrawal symptoms
- Short half-life: repeat doses or an infusion may be needed

## Dosage

- 0.01 mg/kg increments, repeated every 60 sec as needed, up to four further doses
  - Maximum total 0.05 mg/kg or 1 mg, whichever is lower
- Alternative: 200 mcg aliquots

## Mechanism of action

- Competitive benzodiazepine antagonist
- Some agonist activity
- Possible inverse agonist activity, which may explain seizures in some patients

## Effects

- Onset within 2 minutes

## Adverse effects

- Nausea and vomiting
- Anxiety, agitation
- Seizures, especially in epilepsy or mixed drug overdose
- Re-sedation, as it is shorter-acting than most benzodiazepines: observe for at least two hours (2 h) after use

## Indications

- Reversal of benzodiazepines: excess sedation after minor procedures, benzodiazepine overdose

## Pharmacokinetics

- 50% protein bound
- Extensive hepatic metabolism to inactive compounds, excreted in urine
- Half-life about 1 hour, shorter than many benzodiazepines
