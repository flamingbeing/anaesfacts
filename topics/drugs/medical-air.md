# Medical Air

Medical gas made by blending oxygen and nitrogen; supplied in cylinders or by compressor to the pipeline.

## Presentation

- Cylinders: medical-grade air made by blending oxygen and nitrogen
- Pipeline: hospital supply comes from compressor pumps
  - Compressor intakes should be sited away from exhaust vents

## Administration

- Cylinder pressure is proportional to the amount left
  - Air is stored as a gas only, because its critical temperature is -140°C

## Flashcards
- Q: How is medical air supplied in cylinders?
  - A: Medical-grade air made by blending oxygen and nitrogen
- Q: Why is air stored in cylinders only as a gas?
  - A: Its critical temperature is -140°C
- Q: How does cylinder pressure relate to contents for air?
  - A: Pressure is proportional to the amount left
- Q: Where does hospital pipeline air come from?
  - A: Compressor pumps, with intakes sited away from exhaust vents
