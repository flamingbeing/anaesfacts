# Medical Air

Medical gas made by blending oxygen and nitrogen; supplied in cylinders or by compressor to the pipeline.

## Presentation

- Cylinders: medical-grade air made by blending oxygen and nitrogen
- Pipeline: hospital supply comes from compressor pumps
  - Compressor intakes should be sited away from exhaust vents

## Administration

- Cylinder pressure is proportional to the amount left
  - Air is stored as a gas only, because its critical temperature is -140°C
