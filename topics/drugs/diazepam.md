# Diazepam

Long-acting benzodiazepine (Valium) with active metabolites; slower in onset than midazolam.

## Dosage
- IV sedation: 0.05-0.1 mg/kg, up to 0.25 mg/kg
  - Onset 4-5mins (slower than midazolam); peak immediate
  - Anticonvulsant effect lasts 20-30mins
  - Sedation lasts 2-3hrs; may stretch to 90 hours in the elderly or with hepatic or renal dysfunction
- Oral
  - Onset 30mins, peak 2hrs, duration 3-8hrs
  - Paediatric adjuvant analgesic: 0.025-0.2mg/kg every 6 hours

## Effects
- Some cardiorespiratory depression; respiratory depression is worse with opioids or alcohol
- Like other benzodiazepines, reduces the MAC of other anaesthetic agents

## Pharmacokinetics
- High lipid solubility: good oral absorption and fast central effect
- About 95% protein bound (albumin)
- Hepatic oxidation to desmethyldiazepam, oxazepam and temazepam, all active
  - Does not induce hepatic enzymes
  - Glucuronides excreted in urine
  - Liver failure and cimetidine prolong its action by slowing metabolism
- Lowest clearance of the common benzodiazepines; half-life greatly prolonged by infusion
- Elimination half-life 20-40hrs
