# Sugammadex

Modified gamma-cyclodextrin that encapsulates aminosteroid neuromuscular blockers (best for rocuronium) and reverses even profound block.

## Presentation
- 100 mg/ml solution in 2 or 5 ml vials
- Shelf life 3 years

## Administration
- IV, given undiluted

## Dosage
- Depends on depth of block
  - Moderate block (TOF count = 2 visible): 2 mg/kg
  - Deep block (post-tetanic count PTC = 1-2): 4 mg/kg
  - Immediate reversal of profound block (e.g. straight after 1.2 mg/kg rocuronium): 16 mg/kg; needs several vials
- The emergency dose is a key selling point: makes rocuronium a possible alternative to suxamethonium for RSI

## Mechanism of action
- Selective relaxant binding agent (SRBA): encapsulates aminosteroid non-depolarising blockers in plasma
- The resulting concentration gradient draws blocker out of the neuromuscular junction into plasma
- Specific action, so unlike anticholinesterases it has no predictable autonomic side effects
- Cyclodextrins
  - Rings of 6 (α), 7 (β) or 8 (γ) sugars
  - Hydrophobic inside, hydrophilic outside: hydrophobic drugs complex inside, raising solubility and bioavailability
  - Other uses: removing cholesterol from foods, complexing fragrances and toxins

## Adverse effects
- Hypersensitivity reactions (an ongoing FDA concern)
- Severe bradycardia, very rarely
- Interactions
  - Toremifene (a selective oestrogen receptor modulator) or IV fusidic acid can displace rocuronium or vecuronium, delaying return to T4:T1 ratio >0.9
  - Binds progesterone: a bolus equals one missed oral contraceptive pill; with non-oral hormonal contraception use extra contraception for 7 days

## Contraindications
- All debated
- Severe renal impairment (eGFR <30)
  - Possible recurarisation if the renally excreted complex dissociates (though it is very stable)
  - Not recommended in end-stage renal failure because safety is unknown, but often used
- Pregnancy: binds progesterone, which maintains pregnancy
- Breastfeeding
  - Transfer to the infant probably low (hydrophilic, poorly absorbed orally)
  - SOAP advises anticholinesterase reversal instead; otherwise pump and discard milk for the first 12–14 h after surgery
- Children <2yo
  - FDA approved from 2 years, with adult-like dosing
  - Under 2 years: off-label (immature neuromuscular junction, different receptor morphology)

## Indications
- Only use: reversal of rocuronium or vecuronium block
- Most effective for rocuronium; also reverses vecuronium; binds pancuronium weakly

## Pharmacokinetics
- Volume of distribution 11–14 litres; linear kinetics at clinical doses
- Not metabolised; excreted only by the kidneys
- Effective half-life 2.5 hours
