# Paracetamol in children

The commonest analgesic in children; also an antipyretic. Prefer IV over rectal when oral is not possible.

## Administration
- Rectal absorption is slow and bioavailability varies with patient and preparation
  - Plasma levels are often subtherapeutic even after 30 mg/kg
- When the oral route is unavailable, IV is preferable to rectal

## Dosage
- Oral: load 20 mg/kg, then 15 mg/kg 4-hourly
- IV: 15 mg/kg 6-hourly
- Rectal: load 40 mg/kg (20 mg/kg in neonates), then 20 mg/kg 6-hourly
- Maximum daily dose
  - Oral and rectal, over 3 months of age: 90 mg/kg/day (4 g/day in adolescents)
  - Oral and rectal, neonates and infants up to 3 months: 60 mg/kg/day
  - IV: 60 mg/kg/day
- Neonates and infants
  - Under 1 month: 7.5 mg/kg
  - Under 3-6 months (as recorded): oral or IV 10 mg/kg Q6H

## Pharmacokinetics
- In neonates, sulphation (not glucuronidation) is the main metabolic pathway
