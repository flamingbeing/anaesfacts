# Metaraminol

Synthetic amine vasopressor with direct and indirect sympathomimetic action, mainly α1 with some β activity.

## Presentation

- Clear solution, 10 mg/ml

## Dosage

- IV bolus of 0.5–2 mg is usually enough

## Mechanism of action

- Direct and indirect sympathomimetic
- Mainly α1-adrenoceptor agonist; some β-adrenoceptor activity remains

## Effects

- SVR increases, so blood pressure rises
- Cardiac output often falls against the higher SVR, despite the β activity
- Coronary blood flow increases (indirect mechanism)
- Pulmonary vascular resistance increases, so pulmonary artery pressure rises

## Indications

- Hypotension from spinal or epidural anaesthesia
