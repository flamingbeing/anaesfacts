# Perioperative Anticoagulants and Antiplatelets

Weigh the patient's thromboembolic risk against the procedure's bleeding risk, then decide whether to continue, stop, bridge or defer; involve cardiology and haematology.

## Quick timings
- Aspirin or clopidogrel (Plavix): stop 7 days
- Warfarin: stop 5 days, check INR
- UFH: stop 4-6hrs, check APTT
- Enoxaparin (Clexane): stop 12h (prophylactic) or 24h (therapeutic)
- Apixaban: stop 48-72hrs

## Patient thromboembolic risk
- High (>10%/yr)
  - Valves: mechanical mitral or tricuspid; old caged-ball or tilting-disc; multiple valves; stroke or TIA <3 months
  - AF: CHA2DS2-VASc 7–9; rheumatic; stroke or TIA <3 months
  - VTE: recent VTE <3 months; severe thrombophilia (triple-positive antiphospholipid syndrome, protein C, S or antithrombin deficiency)
  - Other: EF <30%; intracardiac thrombus <3 months; recent ACS; clot during previous bridging
- Moderate
  - Bileaflet AVR plus 1 risk factor (AF, stroke, hypertension, diabetes, heart failure, age >75)
  - AF with CHA2DS2-VASc 5-6
  - VTE 3–12 months ago, active cancer, recurrent VTE
- Low
  - Bileaflet AVR with no risk factors
  - AF with CHA2DS2-VASc 1-4
  - VTE >12 months ago

## Procedure bleeding risk
- High: neuraxial block; intracranial, spinal or posterior eye surgery; major cavity or cancer resection
- Minimal: cataract, minor dental, superficial skin surgery, diagnostic endoscopy

## General strategy
- Low bleeding risk: continue; optimise local haemostasis
- High bleeding risk: defer, or stop drugs, or stop drugs and bridge if thrombotic risk is high
- Preoperative: refer to cardiology and haematology
- Intraoperative: patient blood management, tranexamic acid, ROTEM
- Postoperative: restart bridging heparin and anticoagulation
- Consider an IVC filter

## Antiplatelets
- Recent coronary stents: see IHD and stents
- Aspirin: stop 7 days before
- Clopidogrel
  - Stop 5 days before if high cardiac risk
  - Stop 7 days before if low cardiac risk and high bleeding risk
- Dipyridamole: stop 10 days before
- Ticlopidine: stop 14 days before
- Eptifibatide, tirofiban: stop 4-8h before
- Abciximab: stop 24-48h before
- Restart 24h after surgery

## Warfarin
- Urgent surgery: PCC and vitamin K
- Semi-urgent: vitamin K IV 1–2.5 mg or oral 2.5–5 mg; reverses over 1-2 days
- Elective
  - Stop 5 days before
  - Check INR 1 day before; target INR <1.5
  - Consider oral vitamin K 1-2mg if INR > 1.5
  - Neuraxial block: proceed when INR is normal, < 1.2 (ASRA)

### Bridging
- Only for high thromboembolic risk; for warfarin only, not DOACs
- Subcutaneous enoxaparin 1-1.5mg/kg BD (therapeutic) or 40mg BD (intermediate)
  - Stop warfarin 5 days before
  - Start LMWH 3 days before (or when INR < 2)
- Alternative: IV UFH titrated to 1.5-2x APTT
  - For severe renal impairment or dialysis (LMWH unsuitable)
- Stopping heparin before surgery
  - Therapeutic LMWH: stop 24h before
  - IV UFH: stop 4-6h before and check APTT

### Restarting after surgery
- UFH and LMWH act fast, so do not restart too early
- Restart warfarin 12-24h after surgery with LMWH overlap (unless high bleeding risk)
  - INR takes 5 days to rise > 2 after restarting
  - Stop UFH or LMWH once INR is therapeutic for 2 days in a row

## DOACs
- No bridging needed
- Stopping time depends on the drug and renal function
- Apixaban (e.g. 5mg BD)
  - High bleeding risk (and neuraxial): stop 72hrs (3 days) before
  - Low bleeding risk: stop 48hrs (2 days) before
  - CrCl<30: add another 24hrs (AHA 2024)

## Emergency reversal
- Antiplatelets: 1 unit of platelets (CSP)
- Warfarin: PCC plus vitamin K
- DOACs: andexanet alfa or idarucizumab, or PCC

## Flashcards
- Q: When to stop warfarin before elective surgery, and the INR target?
  - A: 5 days before; INR <1.5 checked 1 day before (normal, < 1.2, for neuraxial per ASRA)
- Q: When to stop apixaban before high bleeding risk surgery or neuraxial?
  - A: 72hrs (3 days); add 24hrs if CrCl<30
- Q: When to stop therapeutic LMWH and IV UFH before surgery?
  - A: LMWH 24h; UFH 4-6h with an APTT check
- Q: Who needs bridging?
  - A: High thromboembolic risk patients on warfarin only, not DOACs
- Q: Therapeutic enoxaparin bridging dose?
  - A: 1-1.5mg/kg BD subcutaneously, starting 3 days before surgery
