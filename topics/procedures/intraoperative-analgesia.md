# Intraoperative analgesia and nociception monitoring

Why intraoperative analgesia matters, the multimodal options, and how nociception is monitored.

## Rationale

- Blunts the autonomic (sympathetic) response to surgery
- Pre-emptive effect: avoids sensitisation of pain receptors

## Multimodal analgesia options

- Drugs
  - Paracetamol
  - NSAIDs
  - Opioids
- Adjuncts
  - Nitrous oxide
  - Ketamine
  - Lignocaine
  - Dexmedetomidine
  - Magnesium
- Regional anaesthesia
  - Central neuraxial blocks
  - Peripheral nerve blocks

## Monitoring

- Clinical signs of nociception: tachycardia, hypertension, lacrimation
- Nociception monitoring devices
  - Analgesia nociception index (ANI)
  - Surgical pleth index (SPI)
  - NOL index
  - qNOX index
  - Pupillometry
  - Skin conductance
