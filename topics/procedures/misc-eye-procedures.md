# Miscellaneous Eye Procedures

Anaesthetic notes for short eye and eyelid operations, most of which can be done under MAC.

## Pterygium excision and conjunctival autograft
- Usually done under MAC
- Longer than phaco (about 30-45min) and delicate, so surgeons may choose a block

## Entropion repair
- Duration about 1hr; can be done under MAC
- Extensive repairs may need grafts

## Ectropion repair
- Duration about 1hr; can be done under MAC
- Extensive repairs may need grafts

## Ptosis repair
- Usually age-related (aponeurotic dehiscence), but check for an underlying neuromuscular condition (e.g. myasthenia, oculopharyngeal dystrophy)
- Blepharoplasty:
  - Lid position is measured during surgery
  - So done under LA with the patient sitting upright
  - Children usually need GA
  - Discuss with the surgeon how much patient cooperation is needed
  - Duration about 1hr
- Frontalis sling: done under GA

## Eyelid reconstruction
- Can be long if a graft is taken, so GA
- If oral mucosa is harvested, may need an ETT and throat pack

## Flashcards
- Q: What should you look for in a patient presenting for ptosis repair?
  - A: An underlying neuromuscular condition such as myasthenia or oculopharyngeal dystrophy
- Q: Why is ptosis repair by blepharoplasty done under LA with the patient upright?
  - A: Lid position must be measured during surgery, which needs patient cooperation
- Q: How long does pterygium excision with conjunctival autograft take?
  - A: About 30-45min, longer than phaco, so surgeons may choose a block
- Q: What airway is needed if oral mucosa is harvested for eyelid reconstruction?
  - A: An ETT with a throat pack
