# Drug-Induced Sleep Endoscopy (DISE)

Sedate an OSA patient into snoring sleep, with no airway device, so the obstruction can be seen.

## Procedure
- Nasendoscopy during drug-induced sleep to find the site of upper airway obstruction and better target OSA treatment
- Sedation must be deep enough to bring on snoring for a valid assessment
- Once asleep, the endoscopist passes the nasendoscope; the mandible may be pulled forward to see if the airway opens

## Key considerations
- Aim: sedation that produces sleep
  - Propofol, midazolam and dexmedetomidine can all be used
- No airway device can be used
- Day case in OSA patients: avoid long-acting sedatives
- Procedure takes about 15mins; listed for 30mins

## Intraoperative management
- Supine on bed or theatre table, comfortable room temperature, dimmed lights, eyes covered with a paper mask; patient encouraged to sleep
- Monitoring: routine plus BIS
- Equipment: TCI pump for propofol
- Airway: nasal cannula

## Sedation regimens
- Propofol TCI
  - Fast onset, steady levels by TCI, depth guided by BIS
  - Slow technique: start at effect-site 1.5 mcg/ml, increase until the snoring-apnoea cycle starts, then hold that target until the end
  - Rapid technique: start at 2.5 mcg/ml
- Propofol with midazolam
  - IV midazolam (3–5 mg) and propofol (30–50 mg), titrated to sleep
  - Further 20-mg propofol boluses every two minutes to maintain sedation
- Dexmedetomidine
  - Less respiratory depression than propofol, haemodynamically stable, sleep closer to natural sleep
  - 1mcg/kg loading over 10 min, then 0.2- 0.7 mcg/kg/h

## Postoperative management
- Observe in PACU for at least an hour (OSA)

## Flashcards
- Q: Starting propofol effect-site target for the slow and rapid DISE techniques?
  - A: Slow 1.5 mcg/ml (titrate to snoring-apnoea cycle); rapid 2.5 mcg/ml
- Q: Dexmedetomidine regimen for DISE?
  - A: 1mcg/kg over 10 min, then 0.2-0.7 mcg/kg/h
- Q: Propofol-midazolam regimen for DISE?
  - A: Midazolam 3–5 mg plus propofol 30–50 mg, then 20-mg propofol boluses every 2 minutes
- Q: Minimum PACU stay after DISE?
  - A: At least an hour (OSA)
