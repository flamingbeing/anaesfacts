# Cervical Cerclage

Suture placed around the cervix to prevent miscarriage, usually in the 2nd trimester.

## Surgery
- Usually done between the 14th and 26th week
- Emergency cerclage may be needed if the cervix is dilating and membranes are bulging
- Transvaginal or transabdominal approach
- Risks: membrane rupture, infection, haemorrhage, preterm labour

## Preoperative assessment
- Pregnant: take aspiration precautions

## Intraoperative management
- Spinal or GA with RSI
- Lithotomy position
- Regional: block needs to reach above T10
- GTN 50 mcg boluses relax the uterus
  - Lets bulging membranes be reduced

## Postoperative management
- Observe for preterm labour
- Suture usually removed at 38 weeks
- Removal can be done without anaesthesia
