# Parotidectomy

Removal of the parotid gland while preserving the facial nerve: no neuromuscular block during dissection because of EMG nerve monitoring.

## Surgery

- Excision of the parotid, preserving the facial nerve
- Types: superficial, total, radical (facial nerve sacrificed)

## Preoperative assessment

- Careful airway assessment
- Check mouth opening

## Intraoperative management

- Position: supine, head tilted and extended
- Airway: north-facing RAE tube, or reinforced LMA
- Facial nerve EMG monitoring: no neuromuscular block during dissection
  - Suppress respiratory drive with remifentanil, hyperventilation, propofol
  - Otherwise no special technique needed
- Can bleed: avoid hypotension, good IV access

## Postoperative management

- Analgesia
- Watch for rebound hypertension; treat as for neck dissection
- Clonidine in recovery

## Flashcards

- Q: Why avoid neuromuscular blockers during parotid dissection?
  - A: Facial nerve EMG monitoring needs intact muscle response
- Q: How is spontaneous breathing suppressed without relaxant?
  - A: Remifentanil, hyperventilation, propofol
- Q: What separates radical from total parotidectomy?
  - A: The facial nerve is sacrificed in radical
