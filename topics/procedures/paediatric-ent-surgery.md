# Paediatric ENT Surgery

Common, often day-case surgery on a shared airway that the anaesthetist is frequently far from.

## General considerations
- Shared airway: needs very good communication between surgeon and anaesthetist
  - Anaesthetist is often remote from the airway
- Many procedures suit day surgery
- High volume; most done in general hospitals rather than specialist paediatric centres
- Needs sound knowledge of paediatric airway anatomy, physiology and pathology
- Be aware of rarer conditions and syndromes linked to difficult airway and intubation

## Nasal intubation in children
- Use Iliadin nose drops instead of co-phenylcaine spray
- No need to downsize the tube for the nasal route

## Common procedures
- Tonsillectomy and adenoidectomy
- Myringotomy and grommets
- Middle ear surgery: mastoidectomy, tympanoplasty

## Other procedures
- Excision of preauricular sinus
- Face lump excision (e.g. pinna cyst excision)
  - May need an armoured LMA
- Botox injection of salivary glands
  - For hypersalivation

## Flashcards
- Q: What nasal vasoconstrictor is used for paediatric nasal intubation instead of co-phenylcaine spray?
  - A: Iliadin nose drops
- Q: Does the tube need downsizing for paediatric nasal intubation?
  - A: No
- Q: What airway device may a paediatric face lump excision need?
  - A: An armoured LMA
- Q: Why is botox injected into salivary glands in children?
  - A: To treat hypersalivation
