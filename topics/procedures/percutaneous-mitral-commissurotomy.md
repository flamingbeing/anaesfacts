# Percutaneous Mitral Commissurotomy

Balloon dilation of a stenosed mitral valve via transseptal femoral venous access, under GA for TEE guidance.

## Procedure
- TEE measurements
- Groin puncture; wire passed up
- Interatrial septum punctured
- Through the mitral valve
- Balloon inflated: distal balloon, then proximal balloon

## Anaesthetic considerations
- Arterial line for heparin and ACT sampling; consider external jugular access if veins are poor
- GA needed for TEE
- Very brief loss of cardiac output during balloon inflation
- Fluids: keep dry to prevent flash pulmonary oedema
  - Acute severe MR: regurgitant volume backs up into the pulmonary circulation
  - Other causes: iatrogenic ASD from the transseptal puncture; LV diastolic dysfunction unable to take the extra preload from the LA
  - May need furosemide

## Flashcards
- Q: Why is GA usually needed for PTMC?
  - A: For TEE guidance
- Q: Why keep the patient dry during PTMC?
  - A: To prevent flash pulmonary oedema, e.g. from acute severe MR after ballooning
- Q: Causes of pulmonary oedema after PTMC?
  - A: Acute severe MR, iatrogenic ASD, LV diastolic dysfunction
