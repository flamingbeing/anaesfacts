# Hand Replantation and Upper Limb Revascularisation

Urgent, long microsurgical cases that benefit from an upper limb nerve catheter.

## Urgency
- Urgent, highest priority (P1)
- Theatre can be provided first for benchwork on the amputated part

## Duration
- Long, sometimes very long
- Duration depends on:
  - Number of fingers
  - Number of vessels, nerves, tendons and bones
  - Difficulty
- Urinary catheter
- Arterial line

## Regional anaesthesia
- An upper limb nerve catheter is needed
- Insert it before surgery while benchwork is under way

## Flashcards
- Q: What determines the duration of a hand replantation?
  - A: Number of fingers, number of vessels, nerves, tendons and bones, and difficulty.
- Q: When is the upper limb nerve catheter best inserted for a replant?
  - A: Preoperatively, while the surgeons do benchwork on the amputated part.
- Q: What lines are needed for a long replant?
  - A: Urinary catheter and arterial line.
