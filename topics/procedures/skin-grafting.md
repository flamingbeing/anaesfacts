# Skin Grafting

Varied patients (including major burns); GA with LMA and spontaneous breathing or regional; the donor site is the painful part.

## Preoperative assessment
- Wide range of comorbidities
- May have major burns

## Intraoperative management
- GA (LMA) with spontaneous ventilation, or regional anaesthesia
- Blood loss variable
- Donor site is very painful
- Graft types
  1. Full thickness skin graft (FTSG): where thickness, appearance and texture matter (face, hand)
  2. Split skin graft (SSG): can be meshed

## Postoperative care
- LA to the donor site
- Simple analgesics
- NSAIDs

## Flashcards
- Q: Which part of a skin graft operation hurts most?
  - A: The donor site
- Q: When is a full thickness graft chosen over a split skin graft?
  - A: Where thickness, appearance and texture matter, such as face and hand
- Q: Which graft type can be meshed?
  - A: Split skin graft
