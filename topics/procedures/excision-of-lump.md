# Excision of Lump

Usually local anaesthesia or GA with an LMA; the position (and airway) depends on where the lump is.

## Anaesthetic technique
- Local anaesthesia
- GA with LMA

## Positioning
- Depends on the site of the lump
- A lump on the back may need the prone position: use an ETT
- Examples needing prone:
  - Pilonidal cyst
  - Baker cyst

## Flashcards
- Q: What are the usual anaesthetic options for excision of a lump?
  - A: Local anaesthesia, or GA with an LMA.
- Q: What airway is advised if the lump needs prone positioning?
  - A: An ETT.
- Q: Name two lumps that may need prone positioning.
  - A: Pilonidal cyst and Baker cyst.
