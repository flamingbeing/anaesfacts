# Gastric Surgery

Stomach operations including gastrectomy: aspiration, fluid and electrolyte, nutritional and respiratory issues.

## Preoperative issues
- Gastric outlet obstruction
  - Very high aspiration risk: pass a nasogastric tube, then rapid sequence induction
  - Severe dehydration with hypokalaemic, hypochloraemic metabolic alkalosis
- Anaemia from chronic blood loss in malignancy
- Malnutrition
- Acute bleeding leads to hypovolaemia
- Perforation leads to sepsis

## Respiratory risk and analgesia
- High respiratory risk from the upper abdominal incision
- Regional anaesthesia helps the patient breathe deeply
  - Thoracic epidural: gold standard for open thoracotomy or upper laparotomy
  - Alternatives: paravertebral block (PVB), erector spinae plane block (ESP)
- Postoperative non-invasive ventilation (NIV) is contraindicated

## Flashcards
- Q: What is the induction plan in gastric outlet obstruction?
  - A: Pass a nasogastric tube, then rapid sequence induction.
- Q: What acid-base disturbance occurs in gastric outlet obstruction?
  - A: Hypokalaemic, hypochloraemic metabolic alkalosis, with severe dehydration.
- Q: What is the gold-standard analgesia for upper laparotomy?
  - A: Thoracic epidural.
- Q: Which postoperative respiratory support is contraindicated after gastric surgery?
  - A: Non-invasive ventilation.
