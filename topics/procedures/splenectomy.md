# Splenectomy

The indication drives anaesthetic care: haematological disease, cancer staging or emergency bleeding.

## Indications
- Elective: haematological disease and thrombocytopenia, or cancer staging
- Emergency: trauma, ruptured splenic aneurysm, uncontrollable bleeding
  - Needs good IV access and blood products ready

## Disease-specific considerations
- Sickle cell disease: preoperative transfusion may be needed to prevent sickling
- ITP: platelet transfusion usually held until the spleen is out
- Hodgkin's staging: note chemotherapy history, as some drugs affect kidney, lung and heart

## Flashcards
- Q: When are platelets given for splenectomy in ITP?
  - A: Usually after the spleen is removed
- Q: Key preparation for emergency splenectomy?
  - A: Good IV access and blood products available
- Q: Why ask about chemotherapy before splenectomy for Hodgkin's staging?
  - A: Some agents affect kidney, lung and heart function
