# Valve Replacement and Repair

General points for heart valve surgery, including minimally invasive (MIS) approaches.

## Preoperative assessment
- Dental clearance needed before surgery

## Minimally invasive valve surgery
- One-lung ventilation via double-lumen tube (DLT) or bronchial blocker
  - Keep the patient deep until the end: the tube may need to be reinserted (exchanged) over a paediatric bougie
- Position: supine with a shoulder roll under the operative side

## Flashcards
- Q: What must be arranged preoperatively before valve surgery?
  - A: Dental clearance.
- Q: How is one-lung ventilation provided for minimally invasive valve surgery?
  - A: Double-lumen tube or bronchial blocker.
- Q: Why keep the patient deep until the end of MIS valve surgery?
  - A: The tube may need reinsertion over a paediatric bougie.
- Q: How is the patient positioned for MIS valve surgery?
  - A: Supine with a shoulder roll on the operative side.
