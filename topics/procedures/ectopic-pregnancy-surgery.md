# Ectopic Pregnancy Surgery

Laparotomy or laparoscopy to stop bleeding from a ruptured tubal pregnancy.

## Preoperative assessment
- Presentation varies from haemodynamically normal to severe hypovolaemic shock
- Large-bore IV access
- Crossmatch, FBC and coagulation screen on arrival
- If the patient is unstable, get a second anaesthetist to help

## Intraoperative management
- RSI with the patient draped and the surgeon scrubbed and ready to operate
- Ketamine is a good induction agent
- IV resuscitation
- Keep the patient warm

## Postoperative management
- Correct coagulopathy
- PCA

## Flashcards
- Q: When should induction happen for a shocked ruptured ectopic?
  - A: RSI only once the patient is draped and the surgeon is scrubbed and ready.
- Q: Which induction agent suits an unstable ruptured ectopic?
  - A: Ketamine.
- Q: What bloods are sent on arrival?
  - A: Crossmatch, FBC and coagulation screen.
