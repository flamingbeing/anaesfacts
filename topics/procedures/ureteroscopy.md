# Ureteroscopy, Laser Lithotripsy, DJ Stent and RIRS

Short endourology for stones in lithotomy under the image intensifier; usually LMA with fentanyl, but some patients are septic.

## Procedures

- DJ stent insertion or change: quick
- Laser lithotripsy: longer
- Retrograde intrarenal surgery (RIRS)

## Intraoperative management

- Done under the image intensifier
  - Patient is behind a screen and hard to reach
  - Use extension tubing
- Lithotomy position
- Technique
  - LMA
  - Fentanyl alone is usually enough
- Antibiotics: according to urine culture
- Ventilation may need to be paused
  - Give a small dose of atracurium 20mg if not already paralysed
- Some patients are septic and very unwell from an infected stone
  - High-risk patients can sometimes be done under monitored anaesthesia care (MAC)

## Flashcards

- Q: Usual airway and analgesia for ureteroscopy?
  - A: LMA with fentanyl alone
- Q: What to give if ventilation must be paused and the patient is not paralysed?
  - A: A small dose of atracurium, 20mg
- Q: How are antibiotics chosen?
  - A: According to urine culture
