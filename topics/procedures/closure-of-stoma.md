# Closure and Revision of Colostomy or Ileostomy

Local closure of a colostomy or loop ileostomy: short operation, moderate pain, still needs muscle relaxation.

## Overview
- Duration: 0.5–1 hr
- Pain: moderate (++)
- Blood loss: nil
- Position: supine

## Anaesthetic management
- Muscle relaxation still required
- Analgesia options:
  - PCA, with or without a TAP block
  - Low thoracic epidural, with or without a rectus sheath block

## Flashcards
- Q: How long does a local closure of a colostomy or loop ileostomy usually take?
  - A: About 0.5–1 hr
- Q: Does stoma closure need muscle relaxation?
  - A: Yes, it still requires muscle relaxation
- Q: Name regional options for analgesia after stoma closure.
  - A: TAP block (with PCA), or low thoracic epidural with or without rectus sheath block
