# Tension-Free Vaginal Tape

Tape insertion for stress urinary incontinence: a short, minimally painful day-case in lithotomy, often done awake so the patient can cough to set the tape.

## Preoperative assessment

- May have comorbidities
- May be obese

## Intraoperative management

- Lithotomy
- Minimal pain
- GA (LMA), spinal, or local with sedation: discuss with the surgeon
  - Many surgeons want the patient able to cough to adjust the tape, so avoiding GA helps
- NSAIDs

## Postoperative management

- Usually a day case
- Opioids rarely needed

## Flashcards

- Q: Why might the surgeon prefer the patient awake for TVT?
  - A: So the patient can cough while the tape tension is adjusted
- Q: Anaesthetic options for TVT?
  - A: GA with LMA, spinal, or local anaesthesia with sedation
