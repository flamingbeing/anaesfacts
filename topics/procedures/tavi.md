# Transcatheter Aortic Valve Implantation

Catheter valve replacement for frail patients with AS; set up as for bypass, keep SBP >100 before rapid pacing, and expect hypertension after deployment.

## Indications
- Mainly frail elderly patients needing AVR
- Others
  - Porcelain aorta
  - Severe kyphoscoliosis
  - Significant cirrhosis
  - Previous mediastinal radiotherapy

## Approaches
- Transfemoral, percutaneous
  - Usually MAC or LA if TOE not needed
  - Usually GA if TOE needed
- Transapical, via mini-thoracotomy
  - Needs more analgesia
  - Blood ready in the angio suite for the apical ventriculotomy
  - Consider cell salvage

## Preoperative preparation
- Set up as for cardiopulmonary bypass

## Intraoperative management
- Check with the cardiologist before using the right radial artery
- Critical steps
  - Wire across the valve: can cause VT or VF
  - Balloon valvotomy: causes sudden AR; can cause PEA
  - Valve deployment: valve can embolise if the patient moves or during CPR
- Intermittent rapid ventricular pacing, for valvotomy and valve placement
  - Before pacing: SBP >100mmHg; adrenaline ready in case of myocardial stunning
- Stop ventilation while the valve is positioned to keep the field still
- After valve insertion
  - Marked hypertension possible: have esmolol and GTN ready
  - LVOT obstruction can occur from sudden afterload reduction and higher stroke volume; treat with fluids and an alpha-agonist

## Postoperative care
- Early extubation is possible

## Complications
- Valve regurgitation: minor paravalvular leak in >50%
- Cardiac: any, including tamponade
- Vascular: e.g. rupture, tearing
- Neurological

## Flashcards
- Q: What SBP is wanted before rapid ventricular pacing?
  - A: >100mmHg, with adrenaline ready
- Q: How common is minor paravalvular regurgitation after TAVI?
  - A: More than 50%
- Q: What happens to BP after valve deployment, and what should be ready?
  - A: Marked hypertension; esmolol and GTN
- Q: Treatment of LVOT obstruction after TAVI?
  - A: Fluids and an alpha-agonist
