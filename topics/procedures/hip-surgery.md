# Hip Surgery

Overview of hip operations and the nerve supply that matters for hip analgesia.

## Operations
- Hip fracture surgery (neck of femur, intertrochanteric)
- Total hip replacement

## Innervation for hip joint analgesia
- Anterior hip capsule: articular branches of 3 nerves from the lumbar plexus
  - Femoral nerve
  - Obturator nerve
  - Accessory obturator nerve
- The anterior capsule carries most of the joint's sensory supply
  - Main source of postoperative hip pain
  - Target of the PENG (pericapsular nerve group) block

## Flashcards
- Q: Which nerves supply the anterior hip capsule?
  - A: Articular branches of the femoral, obturator and accessory obturator nerves (3 nerves, lumbar plexus)
- Q: Which part of the hip capsule is the main source of postoperative pain?
  - A: The anterior capsule
- Q: Which block targets the anterior hip capsule?
  - A: The PENG block
