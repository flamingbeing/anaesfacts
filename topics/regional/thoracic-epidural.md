# Thoracic Epidural Anaesthesia and Analgesia

Thoracic epidural placement: choose the level by the dermatomes needed, and use a paramedian approach in the mid and upper thoracic spine.

## Anatomy of the thoracic epidural space

- Borders
  - Anterolateral: pedicles
  - Posterolateral: laminae and ligamentum flavum
- Spinous processes are steeply angled, but less so in the lower thoracic spine
  - Low thoracic: midline approach no harder than lumbar (similar spinous process angle)
  - Mid and high thoracic: the needle must be angled steeply upwards through a small gap, so the midline is harder
- Compared with the lumbar epidural space (from T2 down)
  - More continuous
  - Less fat
  - Valveless veins
  - Nerve roots half the diameter

## Landmarks

- Root (medial end) of the spine of the scapula: T3
- Inferior angle of the scapula: T7

## Level of insertion by surgery

- T2-T4 (upper thoracic): thoracic surgery
- T5-T8 (mid thoracic): upper abdominal surgery
- T9-T11 (low thoracic): lower abdominal surgery

## Spread

- Low thoracic epidurals spread more cranially
- High thoracic epidurals spread mostly caudally
- Reason: the epidural space widens progressively lower down
- So insert a high thoracic epidural at the upper end of the incision, and a low thoracic epidural at the middle of the incision

## Paramedian approach

- Palpate the inferior tip of the spinous process above
- Enter 1cm lateral to the spinous process
- Start perpendicular in all planes until the needle contacts the lamina
- Redirect 15deg towards the midline (north-north-west) and 60 deg to the coronal plane
- A rubbery feel as the needle enters the ligamentum flavum
- Loss of resistance syringe

## Flashcards

- Q: Which vertebral level is at the inferior angle of the scapula?
  - A: T7
- Q: Insertion level for an upper abdominal incision?
  - A: T5-T8 (mid thoracic)
- Q: Which way does a high thoracic epidural spread?
  - A: Mostly caudally (the space widens lower down)
- Q: Paramedian thoracic epidural needle angles?
  - A: Enter 1cm lateral; after hitting lamina, redirect 15deg to the midline and 60 deg to the coronal plane
