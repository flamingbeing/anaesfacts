# Thoracolumbar Interfascial Plane Block

Fascial plane block between the paraspinal muscles, more targeted than ESP for spine surgery.

## Overview
- Used for spine surgery; more targeted than the erector spinae plane (ESP) block

## Approaches
- Classical (medial): the plane between multifidus and longissimus
- Modified (lateral): the plane between longissimus and iliocostalis

## Flashcards
- Q: Which plane does the classical TLIP block target?
  - A: Between multifidus and longissimus
- Q: Which plane does the modified TLIP block target?
  - A: Between longissimus and iliocostalis
- Q: Main use of the TLIP block?
  - A: Analgesia for spine surgery
