# Sympathetic Stimulation in Anaesthesia

Causes of sympathetic activation around anaesthesia and its signs.

## Features
- Tachycardia
- Increased contractility (inotropy)
- Increased SVR from vasoconstriction

## Causes
- Preoperative: anxiety
- Laryngoscopy
- Surgical stimulus
  - Inadequate depth
  - Inadequate analgesia
- Hypovolaemia
- Emergence
- Drugs: adrenergic agents
- Postoperative: pain, nausea and vomiting

## Flashcards
- Q: What are the cardiovascular signs of sympathetic stimulation?
  - A: Tachycardia, increased contractility, raised SVR from vasoconstriction
- Q: Name intraoperative causes of sympathetic stimulation.
  - A: Laryngoscopy, surgical stimulus with inadequate depth or analgesia, hypovolaemia, emergence, adrenergic drugs
- Q: Postoperative causes?
  - A: Pain, nausea and vomiting
