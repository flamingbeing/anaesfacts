# Critically Ill ICU Patients Coming to Theatre

System-by-system review, safe transfer, then cautious induction or continuation of ICU sedation.

## Preoperative assessment by system
- Airway
- Cardiovascular: noradrenaline, vasopressin, adrenaline infusions
- Respiratory: ventilator settings, FiO2
- Gastrointestinal: may not need fasting if intubated (unless high aspiration risk or airway surgery)
- Renal: volume status and fluid input and output targets
- Haematology: latest bloods
- Infection: continue antibiotics
- Lines

## Intraoperative management
- Transfer with emergency drugs; consider paralysis for transfer
- May still need a large-bore IV
- Induction
  - Often already intubated: deepen with volatile or continue the propofol infusion
  - Not intubated: slow titrated induction with preoxygenation
  - Emergency drugs ready for resuscitation
- Maintenance: TIVA if already on a propofol infusion
- Analgesia: an existing fentanyl infusion can serve as the long-acting opioid

## Flashcards
- Q: Does an intubated ICU patient need to be fasted for surgery?
  - A: Often not, unless high aspiration risk or airway surgery
- Q: Induction for an unintubated critically ill patient?
  - A: Slow titrated induction with preoxygenation and emergency drugs ready
- Q: Maintenance option if the patient arrives on a propofol infusion?
  - A: Continue as TIVA
