# Patient Social Profiling

Social circumstances that change perioperative risk and planning, and when to involve the medical social worker (MSW).

## Young women

- High PONV risk: consider TIVA
- High baseline anxiety

## Other clinical groups

- Frail elderly: see elderly and frailty topic
- Vasculopaths: see vascular patients topic

## Socially isolated or living alone (e.g. isolated elderly)

- May be unsuitable for day surgery
- High risk of medication errors after discharge
- Early surgical complications may go unrecognised
- Alcohol misuse
- Needs a caregiver: involve MSW

## Primary caregivers (e.g. mother of an infant, man caring for a spouse with dementia)

- High risk of leaving early against advice or refusing needed admission
- Early MSW input

## Socioeconomically deprived

- Undiagnosed chronic illness
- Cost worries: MSW
- Homelessness
- Alcohol misuse

## Migrant workers and language barriers

- Language barrier affects consent and assessment: use an interpreter
- Unreported baseline health problems
- Financial worries, employer insurance
- Family overseas

## Private, paying or highly educated patients

- Unrealistic expectations about timelines
- Hyper-vigilant about side effects: counsel proactively
- Unverified or misapplied online information: evidence-based discussion
- Intellectualised anxiety: share control

## Overseas patients (medical tourism)

- Incomplete history: reconcile carefully with overseas hospitals
- No local support after discharge
- Fixed flight dates: plan discharge early, balanced against mandatory no-fly periods

## Doctors and medical families

- VIP syndrome: stick strictly to usual protocols
- Performance anxiety: keep clear professional boundaries

## Institutionalised or incarcerated patients

- Consent and autonomy
- Risk to staff
- Logistics: guards must stay present; handcuffs complicate line placement
- Blood-borne viruses

## Other groups

- Substance misuse: see substance abuse topic
- Religious or cultural objectors (e.g. Jehovah's Witnesses): see Jehovah's Witness topic
- Intellectual disability or dementia: see those topics
- Professional voice users and athletes: very sensitive to specific complications (e.g. vocal cord injury)

## Flashcards

- Q: Perioperative risks of patients who live alone?
  - A: Unsuitable for day surgery, post-discharge medication errors, missed early complications
- Q: Key risk for primary caregivers admitted for surgery?
  - A: Leaving early against advice or refusing admission; involve MSW early
- Q: Main issue for overseas medical-tourism patients?
  - A: Incomplete history and fixed flight dates; plan discharge early within no-fly limits
